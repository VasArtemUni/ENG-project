Questions

Does this replace the vet?

No. PawPulse spots changes and tells you when to book a visit. Only a vet can diagnose.

Is the collar safe to wear all day?

It’s lightweight, built for 24/7 wear, and sized to your dog’s weight.

What if my dog won’t use the bed?

It’s a soft orthopedic bed. If your dog doesn’t take to it within 30 days, you get a refund.

How long until it learns my dog?

A personal baseline takes about 2 weeks. Alerts get sharper every week after.
